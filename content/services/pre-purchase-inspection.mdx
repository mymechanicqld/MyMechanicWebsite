## When to get one

Any time you are paying real money for a used car, and especially when:

- Buying privately on Facebook Marketplace, Carsales or Gumtree, where there is no statutory warranty
- The car has been sitting unused for a while
- It is a 4WD or ute that has been used hard, for towing, off-road or on a building site
- You cannot easily get the car to a workshop yourself

## What's included

The inspection covers:

**Under the bonnet**

- Engine condition, leaks, mounts, drive belt and tensioner
- Cooling system inspection (a cooling-system pressure test can be added for an extra fee, just ask when you book)
- Battery state-of-health and alternator output

**Underneath**

- Transmission, drivetrain, CV joints, differential
- Suspension components, ball joints, bushes, shocks
- Steering rack, tie-rod ends, power-steering condition
- Brakes (pad condition at all four corners)
- Exhaust system and catalytic converter condition
- Underbody rust check, especially on the chassis rails

**Body and interior**

- Body panel alignment (check for previous accident repair)
- Paint condition
- All controls: lights, indicators, dash and windows
- Interior condition, seat-belt operation, airbag indicator history
- Tyres, including spare, and wheel condition

**On the road**

- Cold start and warm-up behaviour
- Drive cycle including a freeway run where possible
- Brake feel under hard stop
- Transmission shift quality under load
- Any unusual noises or vibrations

You get a written report with photos, the road-test results, and a plain-English summary of what we would buy, what we would negotiate, and what we would walk away from.

**1. Tell us about the car.** Call **0451 159 954** any time, 24/7, or use the [quote form](/book/?service=pre-purchase-inspection) with the make, model, year, asking price, where the car is, and a link to the listing.

**4. Written report.** Emailed the same day, with photos, so you can walk away, negotiate or buy with confidence.
