## Signs you need a new battery

- The engine cranks slowly, especially on cold mornings
- Headlights dim when the engine is idling
- The battery warning light comes on
- A start-stop car keeps refusing to stop the engine in traffic
- You have needed a jump-start recently

If the battery is over three years old and any of these are happening, replace it before it leaves you stranded.

## What's included

- A test of the battery and charging system first, so we know the battery is the real fault
- A quality replacement battery that meets or exceeds OEM specifications, sized for your vehicle
- Terminals cleaned and re-greased
- Electrical settings reset where your car needs it
- The old battery taken for recycling

If the battery is fine and the fault is the alternator or something draining it, we tell you and quote that work separately. We do not replace a healthy battery.
