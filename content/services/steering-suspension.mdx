## Signs you need this

- A clunk, thud or knock over speed bumps or rough roads
- The car feeling floaty, or taking several bounces to settle
- Tyres wearing more on one edge than the other
- The steering wheel off-centre, or wandering at highway speed
- The car nose-diving heavily under brakes

Worn parts put extra load on the parts around them, so a small repair done early usually costs less than a larger one later.

## What we replace

- **Sway-bar links and bushes**, usually the first parts to wear
- **Ball joints**, the most common source of clunks
- **Control-arm bushes**, which absorb road shock and locate the wheel
- **Tie-rod ends**, which join the steering rack to the wheel
- **Shocks and struts**, replaced in pairs across an axle
- **Strut top mounts**, replaced with the struts
- **Coil springs**, only when broken or sagging

We use OEM or quality aftermarket parts that meet or exceed manufacturer specifications.
