## When to get your brakes checked

Book a check if you notice any of these:

- A squeal when you pull up, or a grinding noise under harder braking
- A pedal that feels soft or travels further than it used to
- The car pulling to one side when you brake
- A brake warning light on the dash

Worn pads damage rotors, and worn rotors damage calipers, so an early check usually means a smaller bill.

## What's included

A standard brake job from us includes:

- Wheels off and a full inspection of the brake hardware
- OEM or quality aftermarket pads, sized for your vehicle
- New rotors when they are worn, scored or warped
- Caliper inspection, with slide pins cleaned and re-greased
- Brake fluid top-up
- A road test to bed in the new pads and check pedal feel

If we find anything else, such as a leaking caliper or a worn hose, we stop and give you a fixed quote before doing more. Nothing is added to the bill without your agreement.
