## What's included

**Minor service** (usually every 10,000 to 15,000 km):

- Engine oil change to manufacturer specification
- New oil filter, OEM or equivalent quality
- Tyre pressures checked and adjusted
- Washer fluid and coolant topped up if needed
- Safety inspection of brakes, suspension, lights, belts, hoses and leaks
- Logbook stamped, service reminder reset and itemised invoice emailed

**Major service** (usually every 30,000 to 60,000 km, by make). Everything in a minor service, plus:

- New air filter and cabin filter
- Brake fluid checked, and flushed if due
- Spark plugs on petrol engines, if due
- Differential and transfer-case oil checked on 4WDs
- Battery health test

If we find anything outside the scheduled items, we take a photo and give you a fixed quote first. Nothing is added to the bill without your agreement.

## Your warranty is safe

Under the Australian Consumer Law, your new-car warranty stays in place when the car is serviced to manufacturer specification by a qualified mechanic using appropriate parts. A fully stamped logbook also helps resale value. More detail: [Does a mobile logbook service void your new-car warranty?](/blog/does-a-mobile-logbook-service-void-your-new-car-warranty/)
